# Brief template

Keep it short: a brief that doesn't fit on one screen won't get read. Leave out any section that doesn't apply.

```markdown
# <Name of the thing>

**Outcome:** <what changes when this is done, in one sentence>
**Audience:** <who, and what they already know>
**Direction:** <chosen direction, with its key traits and references>

## In scope
- ...

## Out of scope (for now)
- ...

## Constraints
- <stack / platform / brand / length / deadline / accessibility>

## Decisions
- <question>: <answer>  (asked)
- <assumption>: <default> (assumed, change if wrong)

## Acceptance checklist
- [ ] <specific, checkable item>
- [ ] ...

## Open for later
- <ideas that came up but were deliberately postponed>
```

## What makes a good acceptance item

- Bad: "Looks modern." Good: "Hero uses the chosen serif at 64px+ with one accent colour."
- Bad: "Fast." Good: "Page loads with no layout shift; hero animation runs once, under 800 ms."
- Bad: "Good copy." Good: "Headline says who it's for and what they get, in 10 words or fewer."
