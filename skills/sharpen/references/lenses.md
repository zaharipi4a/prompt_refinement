# Lenses

Each lens lists the dimensions people most often leave out of a request in that field. Use them to
find unknowns, not as a questionnaire: most become defaults, and only a few become fork questions.
If no lens fits, use the closest one together with the core slots in SKILL.md.

## Website / landing page

- **The one action**: what should a visitor do? (sign up, buy, book, read, be impressed)
- **First 5 seconds**: what must a visitor understand right away?
- **Content**: does real copy and imagery exist, or should you write placeholders? Which sections?
- **Aesthetic direction**: 2–3 adjectives plus one reference site. Ask what they *don't* want as well.
- **Brand assets**: logo, colours, fonts, or none yet?
- **Stack and hosting**: plain HTML, React/Next, Astro, Framer, Webflow? Where will it live?
- **Responsiveness**: mobile first? Which device matters most to this audience?
- **Interactivity**: static, light hover states, or rich motion/3D?
- **Edge cases**: dark mode, i18n, accessibility level, SEO, analytics, forms and where their data goes.

## UI / redesign / design change

- **What's wrong now**: in their words: dated, cluttered, unclear, slow, off-brand?
- **What to keep**: what must not change (layout, colours, flows users already know)?
- **Scope**: one component, one screen, or the whole system?
- **Design system**: existing tokens/components to respect, or start fresh?
- **Fidelity**: rough exploration or production-ready?
- **Density and tone**: calm/airy vs. dense/power-user; serious vs. playful.
- **States**: empty, loading, error, long content, many items.

## Motion design / animation

- **Purpose**: guide attention, explain, give feedback, or delight? Each leads to different motion.
- **Personality**: snappy/precise, soft/organic, bouncy/playful, cinematic/slow.
- **Trigger**: on load, on scroll, on hover, on state change, looping?
- **Intensity budget**: subtle polish, or motion as the main feature?
- **Tooling**: CSS, Framer Motion, GSAP, Lottie, Rive, Three.js, After Effects?
- **Performance and accessibility**: low-end devices, `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Reference**: a link or a description of motion they love. Motion is very hard to put into words, so probe early.

## Code / feature / app

- **User story**: who does what, and why?
- **Inputs and outputs**: data shapes, APIs, files.
- **Where it lives**: which module, service or repo; existing patterns to follow.
- **Edge cases and errors**: what happens on bad input, empty states, failures?
- **Done means**: tests? Which ones? Docs? Deployed?
- **Non-goals**: what not to build yet (auth, admin, scale).
- **Constraints**: dependencies allowed, performance, backwards compatibility.

## Writing (copy, posts, emails, docs, essays)

- **Reader**: who, and what they already know or believe.
- **Desired effect**: what should the reader think, feel or do afterwards?
- **Voice**: a sample of their own writing beats any adjective.
- **Length and format**: where it will be published sets most of this.
- **Key message**: if the reader remembers one sentence, which one?
- **No-gos**: words, claims or tones to avoid.

## Research / analysis

- **Decision it feeds**: what will they do differently depending on the answer?
- **Depth**: a quick scan or a thorough review? How recent must sources be?
- **Scope**: geography, period, industry, what to exclude.
- **Output**: a ranked shortlist, a comparison table, a narrative report, one recommendation?
- **Trust**: which sources count, and which don't?

## Data / charts / dashboards

- **Question**: which question should the chart answer at a glance?
- **Audience**: an executive's quick look or an analyst exploring?
- **Data**: where it comes from, how fresh, how dirty.
- **Comparison**: over time, between categories, against a target?
- **Action**: what should someone do when the number moves?

## Product / strategy / business idea

- **Problem**: whose pain is it, how often does it happen, how bad is it?
- **Current alternative**: what do people do today instead?
- **Bet**: what must be true for this to work? Which of those is least certain?
- **Smallest test**: the cheapest way to learn whether the bet holds.
- **Constraints**: time, money, skills, and what they're willing to give up.

## Decision / personal thinking

- **Real question**: is the stated choice the actual choice?
- **Options**: are there options they haven't listed (including "neither" or "later")?
- **Criteria**: what matters, ranked? What would they regret?
- **Reversibility**: is it a one-way or a two-way door?
- **Deadline**: when must they decide, and what does waiting cost?
